The first iteration of the freshly baked Orchard Cup (OC), has closed the gates and hereby will announce the winners!

![](/wiki/shared/news/2026-08-31-orchard-cup-2026/banner.jpg)

OC 2026 [started about 2 months ago](https://osu.ppy.sh/home/news/2026-08-31-orchard-cup-2026) at the end of August. It takes heavy inspiration from the renowned Newspaper Cup, and aimed to bring new motivated osu!catch mappers to the scene. Even though osu!catch is usually the smallest community out of the 4 modes, we recieved an huge amount of participation and interest and a lot of amazing submissions.

Participants were tasked with mapping any song from the Featured Artist library to compete, and we recieved a total of 56 valid submissions for this iteration. Without further ado, let's now take a look at our top 3 entries as deemed by our judges!
