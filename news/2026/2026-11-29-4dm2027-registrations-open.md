After a hiatus of 2 years, the 7th edition of the 4 Digit osu!mania World Cup is all set and ready for more key-smashing action!

![](/wiki/Tournaments/4DM/2027/img/banner.jpg)

With the [osu!mania World Cup 2026](/wiki/Tournaments/MWC/2026_4K) now behind us, it's time for the rising stars of the osu!mania community to take to the stage! After a 2 year pause, we are excited to welcome the new generational prodigies to the world stage for another iteration of the 4 Digit osu!mania World Cup. As always, there is no fun to be had in the same tournament year after year, so as per usual, we are introducing a handful of changes to make this iteration that much more exciting to watch — and play.

## What's new

We are keeping the catch-up mechanic introduced during [4DM2024](/wiki/Tournaments/4DM/2024). When a team reaches half the amount of points required to win a given match, the opponent will be required to ban an additional map from the pool. However, in contrast to 4DM2024 the amount of protects and bans will be equalised this year. Meaning an additional protect will be issued to teams at the start of a match to prevent their favourite skillsets from getting banned out, and increasing the possibilities for team composition.

Additionally, to help create further possibilities and opportunities, [4DM2027](/wiki/Tournaments/4DM/2027) will see Hard Rock getting introduced to the mappools. Ever thought your accuracy was good? Now is the time to prove it! Hard Rock picks will not be limited to your standard Rice accuracy charts either, as this tournament will also feature Long Note maps with a Hard Rock requirement. 

Each mappool will see a Rice and Long Note chart with Hard Rock. This change will also be included in the Qualifiers mappool, with our stage 5 and 6 picks being Rice- and Long Note-focused Hard Rock picks respectively. Similar to last edition, teams will be able to select their Qualifier playing order beforehand, so choose well!

## How do I register?

[![](/wiki/Tournaments/4DM/2027/img/register.png)](https://forms.topecnz.net/f/ankw5yj)

Players who sign up and meet the following criteria will be eligible to participate:

- **You must be within the 4K rank range of #1,000-9,999** both when you submit your registration and until the end of the registration phase.
- **You must not have violated the [osu! community rules](/wiki/Rules) in the past 12 months.**
- **You must not exceed the global osu!mania rank of #250.**
- **You must not have been a part of a former winning 4DM team.** 4DM1 winners are excluded from this.
- **You must not have been a part of a top 16 team in [MWC 4K 2026](/wiki/Tournaments/MWC/2026_4K).**
- **Join the official [Discord server](https://discord.gg/6gsHEvhv9E) for 4DM2027.**

To keep up with the tournament's latest announcements, we encourage joining the [4DM Discord server](https://discord.gg/6gsHEvhv9E), checking the [forum post](https://osu.ppy.sh/community/forums/topics/2266894), and reading the [wiki article](/wiki/Tournaments/4DM/2027) for more information. We cannot wait for 4DM to commence, and we hope you will be a part of it!
